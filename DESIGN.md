# Covet Campette: approved concept and open engineering questions

Status: **Concept approved; specifications not yet measured.**

## Fixed vehicle design
- Fictional BeamNG.drive baby Class C motorhome using the **vanilla Ibishu Covet front half**, retaining its recognizable hood, fascia, windshield, front doors, dashboard, and front drivetrain packaging.
- Original rounded 1960s Shasta-inspired canned-ham camper shell, not a copied Shasta mesh or an intact trailer attached to a car.
- Camper wider and taller than the front cab, with a blended coachbuilt transition and a compact over-cab sleeping bunk.
- **Real walkthrough** between the driver's area and camper living space, no solid partition.
- Vintage ribbed aluminum look, cream/turquoise initial color concept, side entry door, camper windows, and a functional interior planned.
- Swooping integrated rear wheel arches with **single tucked rear wheels** on each side; no dual rear wheels on base model.
- Distinctive small Shasta-inspired rear corner wings.
- Reinforced automotive rear suspension, FWD standard with optional AWD proposed for later approval and feasibility investigation.

## Engineering not yet verified
Exact Covet JBeam measurements and cut line; roof height/headroom; floor and wall structure; axle donor and capacity; camper mass and front/rear weight distribution; powertrain load capacity and cooling; chassis reinforcements and joint; doorway and walkthrough clearance; wheel track and suspension travel; interior layout; rear drive layout and AWD donor; modeling workflow. Concept art is not engineering geometry.

## Quality bar
Believable quirky 1980s coachbuilt appearance; integrated motorhome rather than a trailer connection; original exterior with proper door/window openings; not an unapproved generic box, placeholder or copied model. Physical JBeam, collisions and visible geometry must align.

## Proposed first technical milestone: NOT AUTHORIZED
Read-only inspection of local Covet and candidate vanilla rear suspension and AWD donors. Measure critical geometry, plan structural load paths and full walkthrough, assess preliminary mass/handling and credible FWD operation, report AWD possibilities and blockers. Present a decision-ready assessment **before** creating or modifying vehicle geometry.
